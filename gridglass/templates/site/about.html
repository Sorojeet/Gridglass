About Gridglass

Gridglass is a set of simple spreadsheet tools that each do one job well, made to work as nicely on a phone as on a computer.

Why it exists

Cleaning or reshaping a spreadsheet usually means a big desktop program, formulas and a lot of scrolling. That's hard on a phone, which is where many people receive their files today, from WhatsApp, email or a work app.

Gridglass turns each common job into one small tool: remove duplicates, filter, sort, stack files, make a pivot table, convert to PDF and {{ tools|length - 6 }} more. You choose a file, pick a few options, see a live preview and download the result. Tools can be chained, every step can be edited or undone, and steps you use often can be saved as a recipe.

How your files are handled

  • Files are read on our server only to run the tools you choose.
  • They are deleted automatically after {{ retention }} minutes, or straight away when you tap Clear or Start over.
  • They are never sold, shared or used for anything else.
  • Ads appear only on information pages like this one, never in the app where your data is shown.
